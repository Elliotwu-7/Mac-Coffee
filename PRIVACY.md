# Mac Coffee Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Mac Coffee does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or backend service.

Mac Coffee reads the Mac's current power-source and battery percentage locally so it can enforce the user-selected low-battery cutoff. This information remains on the device.

The Direct edition contacts its configured HTTPS update feed only through Sparkle's signed update mechanism during scheduled or manual update checks. Its optional MCP integration communicates locally between the selected MCP client and the running Mac Coffee process. MCP credentials remain in Keychain, and the bounded activity view remains in memory; neither is sent to a server. The Mac App Store edition contains no Sparkle or MCP components and receives updates through the App Store. Launch at Login is enabled only after an explicit user choice and is managed by macOS.

Questions may be filed at <https://github.com/rekurt/Mac-Coffee/issues>.
