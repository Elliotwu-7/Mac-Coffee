Mac Coffee is a lightweight native menu-bar utility that keeps your Mac awake only when you choose.

Pick Keep Mac Awake or Keep Display Awake, then run the session for 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4, or 8 hours—or indefinitely. Change the duration at any time, or choose Off to release the wake request immediately.

Protect a MacBook battery with a configurable 10–30% cutoff (15% by default), optionally start Mac Coffee at login, and receive clear local notifications when a session ends or battery protection stops it.

Switch instantly between System, English, Russian, German, French, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish. The panel, Settings, About, dialogs, notifications, errors, countdowns, and accessibility labels update without restarting the app or interrupting an active session.

Mac Coffee uses public, process-owned macOS power assertions. It needs no administrator helper, account, analytics, advertising, or backend and collects no data.

Mac Coffee prevents idle sleep only. Manual Sleep, closing a MacBook lid, shutdown, restart, and macOS thermal or safety decisions always remain effective.
